#### Q1: "Humare paas already DotPe link (roadstove.dotpe.in) hai, toh naye website ki kya zaroorat?"

#### Q5: "Hume technology ki zyada jankari nahi hai, kya ise chalana mushkil hoga?"
**Ans:** Bilkul nahi! Aapko koi coding ya technical kaam nahi karna hai. Hosting, domain, server, maintenance sab hum sambhalte hain. Aapka kaam sirf counter par phone check karna aur customer ko garam-garam khana serve karna hai.

**Ans:** Bilkul ZERO hidden charges! Na koi per-order commission, na koi unexpected bill. Setup fee ek baar deni hai, aur hosting/domain 1 saal baad normal rate pe renew hoti hai.
